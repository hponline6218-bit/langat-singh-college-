ABOUT THE COLLEGE

A Legacy of Education

Langat Singh College is one of the historic educational institutions of Bihar. The college was established on 3 July 1899 in Muzaffarpur.

The institution has developed a rich academic heritage and has played an important role in higher education.

The college promotes knowledge, creativity, tolerance, scientific temper and responsible citizenship among students.
